## The 7 signals your SMB site needs a redesign

You don't need to be an expert to diagnose a site that's no longer performing. These seven signals are objective, measurable, and often ignored for too long.

### 1. The site takes more than 3 seconds to load

Test your site on [PageSpeed Insights](https://pagespeed.web.dev/): a mobile score below 50/100 is a red flag. **53% of mobile visitors abandon a site that takes more than 3 seconds to load**, and the bounce rate jumps by 90% between 1 and 5 seconds. In March 2026, Google lowered its LCP threshold from 2.5s to **2.0 seconds** — classic WordPress sites, averaging 3.2s, are structurally outside current criteria.

### 2. The design isn't mobile-friendly

Over 60% of web traffic comes from mobile devices in 2026. Responsiveness that was "good enough for 2019" is now broken on a significant share of devices: buttons too small, unreadable text, forms impossible to fill out — every friction point is a lost conversion.

### 3. Bounce rate exceeds 70%

2025-2026 benchmarks place the average bounce rate for a showcase site between **40% and 60%**. Beyond 70%, visitors aren't finding what they're looking for — or the experience discourages them before the first paragraph.

### 4. Zero leads in 3 months

Zero completed forms in 3 months despite traffic signals a structural problem: missing call-to-action, form too long, lack of trust signals (reviews, legal notices, SSL).

### 5. Your site doesn't appear on Google for your business keywords

Absent from the top 30 results on your target queries in Google Search Console? Often a sign of accumulated technical debt: duplicate titles, thin content, missing structured data, crawl issues.

### 6. The technology is outdated

Flash, a CMS with no updates in 2 years, PHP below 8.1: as much a security risk as an SEO handicap. Google penalizes known vulnerabilities from 2024.

### 7. Your competitors have redesigned theirs

Comparison happens in 50 milliseconds according to UX studies: competitor redesigned, your site dates from 2020, customers lost before the first conversation.

## Complete redesign vs optimization: how to choose?

A redesign isn't always the right answer. **Optimization is enough** if the site is under 3 years old, has a sound SEO architecture, and the problem is targeted (uncompressed images, poorly configured cache, one page with a bad bounce rate): it costs **€500 to €2,000** and fixes 80% of the problem within weeks. **A redesign is necessary** if the site is over 4 years old with significant technical debt, a dated design, a technology blocking evolution, or structurally blocked SEO.

| Criteria | Optimization | Redesign |
|---|---|---|
| Site age | < 3 years | > 4 years |
| Typical budget | €500 – €2,000 | €3,000 – €15,000 |
| Timeline | 2 – 4 weeks | 6 – 16 weeks |
| SEO risk | Low | High if poorly prepared |
| Result | Partial improvement | Complete transformation |
| Architecture | Kept | Rethought |

Basic rule: if optimization solves 70% of the problem, do it. Otherwise, invest in the redesign.

## Website redesign checklist: 15 points to validate

A poorly prepared redesign can lose 50 to 80% of organic traffic within days. The points serious agencies systematically check:

**Before starting** — ✅ 1. Complete audit (Screaming Frog crawl, GSC export, list of active URLs); ✅ 2. Business objectives (target leads/month, conversion pages, target revenue); ✅ 3. Competitive analysis (keywords captured, converting designs); ✅ 4. Content inventory (pages to keep, migrate, merge, remove); ✅ 5. 301 redirect plan (every changing URL documented before launch).

**During development** — ✅ 6. Clean URL architecture (logical hierarchy, slugs without accents); ✅ 7. Core Web Vitals tested continuously (LCP, INP, CLS); ✅ 8. Mobile-first responsive from the design phase; ✅ 9. Complete SEO markup (title 50-60 chars, description 130-150 chars per page); ✅ 10. Schema.org structured data (Organization, LocalBusiness, FAQ).

**Before going live** — ✅ 11. Performance test (PageSpeed > 80/100 mobile); ✅ 12. Form testing (sending, receiving, automatic acknowledgment); ✅ 13. Internal links checked (no broken links, no orphan pages); ✅ 14. SSL and HTTPS (valid certificate, redirects in place); ✅ 15. Post-launch submission (new sitemap in GSC, indexing request).

## How to choose your agency for a redesign?

The web agency market is opaque. Four non-negotiable criteria: **verifiable references** (live production URLs, before/after GSC results, testimonials reachable by phone); **an SEO audit of the existing site before starting**; **full handover of all access upon delivery** (hosting, domain, source code, CMS, GSC); and **a clear maintenance contract** (bugs, timeframe, rate — the first 3 months post-redesign are critical for SEO).

⚠️ **Warning signs**: a "complete redesign" quote under €1,500 without scope details, no mention of SEO, a timeline under 4 weeks for more than 10 pages, no mockup validation phase, a contract without an intellectual property clause.

For an SMB in the Hauts-de-France region, a **web agency in Lille** often offers better value than a Parisian agency, which charges 30 to 60% more for equivalent services.
