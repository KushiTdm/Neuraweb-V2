## How to integrate an AI chatbot into your website (without breaking the budget)

Integrating an AI chatbot into an existing or new site follows a 5-step process:

**1. Define priority use cases**
Don't try to do everything at once. Start with the 3-5 most frequent questions and the booking flow. For a restaurant, that's: table booking + reminders + menu/hours FAQ. For a hotel: availability + stay FAQ + direct booking.

**2. Connect your existing tools**
The chatbot must be linked to your booking system (TheFork, Zenchef, Google Reserve, Planyo, Mews...) to access real availability. Without this connection, it can only collect requests — not process them.

**3. Feed the knowledge base**
Provide the chatbot with your content: menu, hours, pricing, cancellation policies, FAQ, practical information. The more complete the base, the less the customer escalates to a human.

**4. Configure escalations**
Define when the chatbot hands off to a human (complex requests, complaints, groups of 15+). A good chatbot knows what it doesn't know — and says so.

**5. Test, measure, refine**
The first 30 days reveal unanticipated questions. Continuously enrich the knowledge base. Frequent uncovered questions are content opportunities (blog, FAQ page) not to be missed.

**Deployment time**: 1 to 3 days for a standard SaaS solution, 2 to 3 weeks for a custom chatbot connected to your systems. No site redesign required.

Your AI chatbot contributes to this visibility in two ways:

**Indirectly — engagement signals**
An active chatbot increases time spent on your site, reduces bounce rate, multiplies interactions. These engagement signals are exactly what AI engines consider when evaluating a domain's authority.

**Directly — a GEO content mine**
Every chatbot conversation reveals your customers' real questions. These verbatims are exactly the queries the same users type into ChatGPT or Perplexity. Turn them into:
- Structured FAQ pages (FAQPage schema still useful for LLMs even after Google's deprecation)

- ✅ Fully complete your Google Business Profile (hours, menu, services, photos, reviews)
