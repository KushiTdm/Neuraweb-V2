On 4 October 2026, **one in four VAT-reporting companies in France had still not designated a platform to receive their electronic invoices**, even though reception has been mandatory for all of them since 1 September ([French Ministry of Economy press release, 9 October 2026](https://presse.economie.gouv.fr/facturation-electronique-le-premier-comite-dresse-le-bilan-du-deploiement-et-engage-la-preparation-de-2027/), in French).

**The short answer:** since 1 September 2026, every company subject to VAT in France must be able to receive electronic invoices through an approved platform (*plateforme agréée*, PA). The obligation to issue only applies to SMBs and micro-businesses from 1 September 2027. The Ministry announced that no penalty would be imposed before the end of 2026: that is the window to build a clean reception process, not just to "be connected".

For a restaurant owner, a hotelier or a tradesperson, the issue is not the invoice you send, it is the one you receive. Your biggest suppliers (energy, telecoms, purchasing groups, insurers), when they are large, must already issue electronically: their invoices no longer arrive by e-mail. Here is what changes, what a compliant invoice looks like, how to automate reception with n8n, for what budget, and from what volume it makes no sense.

## Who is affected by e-invoicing in France, and since when?

| Obligation | Who | Date |
|---|---|---|
| Reception | All companies established in France and subject to VAT, including those under the franchise en base | 1 September 2026 |
| Issuing | Large companies and mid-sized companies (ETI) | 1 September 2026 |
| Issuing | SMBs and micro-businesses | 1 September 2027 |

Sources: [service-public.fr](https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/vosdroits/F23208), page verified on 7 August 2026, and the [DGFiP factsheet on issuing electronic invoices](https://www.impots.gouv.fr/sites/default/files/media/1_metier/2_professionnel/EV/2_gestion/290_facturation_electronique/fiches_reforme/fiche-e-invoicing.pdf) (September 2025 version), which specifies that the scheme covers transactions between VAT-registered businesses established in France, including those under the franchise en base. Public bodies must also be able to receive since 1 September 2026 ([AIFE](https://aife.economie.gouv.fr/nos-applications/facturation-electronique-b2b/)).

The 8 October 2026 monitoring committee gives the measure of the rollout: 75% of VAT-reporting companies had designated a reception platform by 4 October, with over 20 million invoices exchanged worth over €70 billion. A quarter of companies had therefore not yet taken that step. The same press release is explicit: "no penalty will be imposed on any company before the end of 2026" (« aucune sanction pour aucune entreprise ne sera prononcée avant la fin de l'année 2026 »). The committee will meet again before year-end to track preparation for September 2027.

## What does this change for a restaurant owner, a hotelier or a tradesperson?

A simple rule: **your purchases from VAT-registered suppliers are electronic invoices to receive; your sales to individuals mostly fall under e-reporting.**

- **Restaurant or hotel**: your purchases (food, drinks, energy, linen, maintenance) are invoices to receive. Your sales to individual customers are not electronic invoices. Your sales to businesses (seminars, groups, agencies) will have to be issued through a platform from September 2027.
- **Tradesperson or construction**: your material suppliers' invoices are to be received now; your own invoices to businesses go through a platform from September 2027.
- **Independent healthcare practice**: operations exempt under articles 261 to 261 E of the French General Tax Code (the medical sector in particular) are excluded from e-invoicing, but your purchases from VAT-registered suppliers may still fall within scope. Have your accountant check your situation before investing.

## What does a compliant electronic invoice look like?

It is not a PDF, it is a file whose data is machine-readable, transmitted through an approved platform.

| Term | What it is |
|---|---|
| Factur-X | Human-readable PDF/A-3 with an embedded XML (CII syntax). Several profiles, from Minimum to Extended |
| UBL and CII | Two pure XML formats, also accepted |
| Approved platform (PA) | Registered operator that issues, receives and transmits your invoices |
| Public invoicing portal (PPF) | Directory of companies and transmission point for data sent to the tax authority: it is not an invoicing platform |
| Lifecycle statuses | Four mandatory ones, fed by the platform (deposited, rejected, refused, cashed); others, optional, complete the tracking |
| AFNOR XP Z12-013 standard | Defines the APIs that connect company information systems to approved platforms |

Sources: [external specifications v3.2 of 30 April 2026](https://www.impots.gouv.fr/specifications-externes-b2b) (XP Z12-013 standard), [DGFiP factsheet](https://www.impots.gouv.fr/sites/default/files/media/1_metier/2_professionnel/EV/2_gestion/290_facturation_electronique/fiches_reforme/fiche-e-invoicing.pdf) (statuses) and [AIFE](https://aife.economie.gouv.fr/nos-applications/facturation-electronique-b2b/) (PPF).

Direct consequence for automation: **a Factur-X or UBL file can be read programmatically.** The SIREN number, invoice number, amounts, VAT and IBAN are fields, not text to guess. No AI is needed to read a structured file, nor for the checks, which rely on business rules. Classic PDF invoices that some suppliers will keep sending until 2027 do require suitable extraction (OCR, possibly AI).

## How do you automate invoice reception with n8n?

The principle: the platform receives, [n8n](/en/blog/automatisation-n8n-guide) checks and files, and you only approve the exceptions. The basic flow takes seven steps and three tools (platform, accounting tool or Drive, messaging):

```text
1. Trigger      : new invoice received on the platform (API, notification or scheduled pull)
2. Retrieval    : Factur-X or UBL file + lifecycle status
3. Reading      : extraction of XML fields (SIREN, invoice no., amounts, VAT, IBAN), no AI
4. Checks       : known supplier? duplicate? IBAN unchanged? total = sum of lines?
5. Filing       : entry in the accounting tool (or Drive) + archiving of the original file
6. Notification : message to the approver (Slack or e-mail) with a link to the invoice
7. Status       : status update on the platform (refusal, optional statuses) if it exposes and allows it
```

**An important caveat.** Each platform exposes its own access. The XP Z12-013 standard defines interconnection APIs, but it does not guarantee that every platform opens them to its customers: APIs, notifications, access rights and status updates depend on the platform and plan chosen, and some are limited to a simple export. Confirming this on your last five received invoices is the first deliverable of our audit, before any quote. On the data side, n8n can be hosted in Europe (OVH, Scaleway or n8n Cloud EU region), as in our [guide to chasing unpaid invoices](/en/blog/agent-ia-relance-factures-pme).

## What happens when the workflow hits a problem?

A workflow without error handling is not a workflow, it is a gamble. Here are the six incidents built into the flow:

| Incident | Workflow reaction | Who steps in |
|---|---|---|
| Duplicate (same supplier, same number) | Blocked, no entry, logged | Nobody, unless disputed |
| Unknown supplier (SIREN not in your list) | Quarantine + alert | You approve or refuse |
| IBAN different from the known one | Payment blocked + alert | Call the supplier on a number you already had |
| Total different from the sum of lines | Quarantine | Your accountant |
| Platform unavailable | Spaced retries, then alert | You or the integrator |
| Unreadable file or unexpected format | Quarantine, original file kept | Integrator |

These checks flag anomalies: they do not guarantee the absence of fraud and do not legally validate the invoice. The Starter pack covers the nominal flow; quarantine, alerts and continuous monitoring belong to the Business pack, which includes monitoring.

## What mistakes should you avoid before the grace period ends?

1. **Waiting for September 2027.** Reception is already mandatory and your large suppliers already issue electronically.
2. **Believing a PDF by e-mail is enough.** For your issued invoices, the obligation does not yet apply to SMBs before September 2027; for the ones you receive from large companies, the free-form PDF is already no longer the rule.
3. **Confusing the PPF with a platform.** The PPF is a directory: you must designate an approved platform, which can be your accounting vendor's.
4. **Automating without checks.** Any IBAN change must trigger a call to the supplier on a number you already had, never a simple confirmation e-mail.
5. **Forgetting archiving.** Accounting records must be kept for 10 years (article L123-22 of the French Commercial Code), in conditions that guarantee their integrity and readability: archive the original file, not just its readable version, and not in a shared folder with no access control.
6. **Reducing GDPR to hosting.** The EU is a good starting point, but you also need a processing agreement, restricted access, defined retention periods and an entry in the record of processing activities (article 30).
7. **Skipping your accountant.** This article is general information, not tax advice: have your process validated.
