In 2026, automating business processes is no longer optional for SMBs — it's a measurable competitive advantage. The question is no longer "should we automate?" but "with which tool?".

Three no-code platforms dominate the market: Zapier, Make (formerly Integromat), and n8n. Each has its advocates, its use cases, and its blind spots. And their pricing pages only tell part of the story — the billing models are so different that a line-by-line comparison is misleading.

After deploying workflows for SMBs across industries — from lead qualification to AI agent orchestration — here's our comparison based on official pricing verified in May 2026.

## Understanding the Billing Models Before Comparing

This is the point most comparison articles miss: Zapier, Make, and n8n don't bill the same thing.

**Zapier bills tasks** — every successful action in a Zap uses 1 task. A 5-action Zap that triggers 500 times per month = 2,500 tasks consumed.

**Make bills credits** — every module execution in a scenario uses 1 credit. A 5-module scenario run 500 times per month = 2,500 credits consumed. Similar logic to Zapier, but different pricing and included volumes.

**n8n bills executions** — one complete workflow run counts as 1 execution, regardless of how many steps it contains. That same 5-step workflow run 500 times per month = 500 executions consumed. This is a major structural advantage for complex workflows.

## The 4 Criteria That Actually Matter for an SMB

**Real cost at 6 months** — Not the entry price, but what you'll actually pay when your usage scales. The billing structure changes everything.

**Ease of onboarding** — How many hours before a non-technical team member can launch their first autonomous workflow?

**AI agent compatibility** — Reasoning loops, persistent memory, multi-agent orchestration: which tool actually supports this in production?

**GDPR / data compliance** — Server location, Data Processing Agreements, data sovereignty. A blocking point in many industries.

## Zapier — The Easiest to Use, the Most Expensive at Scale

Zapier is the most accessible platform on the market. 7,000+ integrations, a drag-and-drop interface masterable in an hour, exhaustive documentation, responsive support.

### Official Pricing (May 2026, annual billing)

- **Free**: 100 tasks/month, 2-step Zaps only
- **Professional**: starting at **$19.99/month** — unlimited multi-step Zaps, task volume is configurable via a slider (750, 1,500, 2,000, 5,000... up to 2M+) with pricing that increases progressively at each tier
- **Team**: starting at **$69/month** — 25 users, shared app connections, SAML SSO
- **Enterprise**: custom pricing

The Agents plan (autonomous AI agents) is **separate**: $33.33/month for 1,500 activities, or free with 400 activities/month.

### The Pricing Trap to Know

The $19.99/month entry price is misleading — that's the minimum tier, covering only 750 tasks/month. At 500 inbound leads with 5 actions each, you consume 2,500 tasks and are forced to move to a higher volume tier. The actual monthly cost for an active SMB consistently exceeds $40–60/month.

### Strengths and Limits

What works: unmatched speed to launch. Connections in 5 minutes, zero training required for simple use cases, the largest integration catalog on the market (7,000+).

The real limits: AI agents in standard Zaps remain basic — no loops, no persistent memory between executions. Data routes through US servers by default, with DPAs available but real legal friction for sensitive or HR data.

### Zapier Verdict

The right choice for getting started quickly on simple linear workflows. Becomes expensive and limiting as volume grows or AI needs go beyond a simple ChatGPT connection.

## Make — The Sweet Spot for Most SMBs

Make offers the best balance of power, accessibility, and price for SMBs in 2026.

### Official Pricing (May 2026, annual billing)

Make bills in **credits**: each module action in a scenario consumes 1 credit.

- **Free**: 1,000 credits/month, max 2 active scenarios, 15-minute minimum interval between runs
- **Core**: **$12/month** — 10,000 credits/month, unlimited scenarios, minute-level scheduling, Make API access
- **Pro**: **$21/month** — 10,000 credits/month + priority execution, custom variables, full-text log search
- **Teams**: **$38/month** — 10,000 credits/month + team management, roles, shareable templates
- **Enterprise**: custom pricing — custom functions, enterprise apps, 24/7 support

Additional credits beyond the included 10,000 can be purchased at tiered rates: 20k, 40k, 80k credits/month with volume discounts.

### Watch the Credit Math

10,000 credits/month means 10,000 module actions executed. An 8-step scenario triggered 500 times per month consumes 4,000 credits — that's 40% of your quota from a single active workflow. For an SMB with 5 to 10 active scenarios at moderate volume, Core at $12/month is typically sufficient. At higher volumes, Make stays competitive: 20,000 credits/month runs around $16/month.

### Strengths and Limits

What works: native error handling, conditional filters, parallel scenarios, and **Make AI Agents** in beta with native Claude, OpenAI, and Mistral connectors. Servers are on AWS EU — GDPR compliance is straightforward to document.

The real limits: Make's terminology (modules, bundles, routes, credits) confuses beginners. Allow 3 to 4 hours of onboarding for a non-technical team member. Make isn't natively designed for AI agents with persistent memory and complex reasoning loops — the AI Agents beta is less powerful than n8n on that front.

### A Real Migration Case

A French e-commerce SMB was paying a Zapier Professional plan exceeding $50/month at its task volume. Migration to Make Core ($12/month): 4 hours of work, no loss of functionality. Annual savings: over $450.

### Make Verdict

Our default recommendation for SMBs. The Core plan at $12/month covers 80% of an active SMB's automation needs. The Pro plan at $21/month adds the team features and advanced logs needed for serious production use.

## n8n — The Platform for AI Agents and Data Sovereignty

n8n is the most technical of the three — and the only one natively designed for autonomous AI agents: loops, persistent memory, conditional decisions, multi-agent orchestration.

### Official Pricing (May 2026)

**Community Edition (self-hosted)**:
- Open-source, 100% free to use
- Actual cost: VPS infrastructure only — €20 to €40/month on OVH, Scaleway, or Hetzner
- Data hosted wherever you decide — full sovereignty

**n8n Cloud plans (annual billing)**:
- **Starter**: **€20/month** — 2,500 executions/month, 1 shared project, 5 concurrent executions
- **Pro**: **€50/month** — 10,000 executions/month, 3 shared projects, 20 concurrent executions, global variables, workflow history, execution log search
- **Business**: **€667/month** — 40,000 executions/month, self-hosted only, SSO/SAML/LDAP, separate environments (dev/staging/prod), Git-based version control
- **Enterprise**: custom pricing

Note: the Business plan is only available self-hosted. For teams without DevOps resources, the Pro cloud plan at €50/month is the right production entry point.

For startups under 20 employees: n8n offers a Startup plan at -50% off Business — worth checking on their dedicated page.

### The Structural Advantage of Execution-Based Billing

This is the most underestimated point in the comparison. On n8n, a 15-step workflow run 500 times per month = 500 executions consumed. The same workflow on Make would consume 7,500 credits. On Zapier, 7,500 tasks.

For simple workflows, the difference is marginal. For AI agents with dozens of reasoning steps, n8n becomes significantly more economical at comparable volume.

### Strengths and Limits

What works: native nodes for Claude API, OpenAI, Mistral, and LangChain handle session memory and reasoning loops in production. On self-hosted Community Edition, data never leaves your infrastructure — often the only viable option for GDPR-sensitive industries.

The real limits: the learning curve is real — 6 to 8 hours for a non-technical team. The Community Edition has no dedicated support (45,000+ member forum, very active, but no SLA). The Business plan at €667/month is significantly more expensive than competitors for teams needing advanced cloud features.

### A Real Deployed Case

Market monitoring stack for a French B2B SMB: n8n Community Edition + Claude API + Search Console, hosted on a Scaleway VPS. Total cost: approximately €120/month (infrastructure + Claude API). Result: 20 minutes of manual work per week for a complete daily brief on market signals.

The same stack on Make Pro ($21/month) would have handled the orchestration logic but not the persistent decision-making loop. On Zapier, this type of agent workflow isn't natively possible.

## Which Tool Fits Your Situation

**Starting out** without technical skills and want to automate 2 to 3 simple processes fast → **Zapier Professional**. Accept the premium price as a learning investment, reassess in 3 months.

**Building 5 to 15 workflows** with a budget to control, sometimes with an AI layer → **Make Core or Pro** ($12 to $21/month). Our default recommendation. Migrating from Zapier? Budget 4 hours of work for savings that pay back in under a quarter.

**Deploying autonomous AI agents**, handling sensitive data (strict GDPR), or planning complex 10+ step workflows at high volume → **n8n**. Community Edition if you have the technical resource to self-host, cloud Pro at €50/month otherwise.

**Under-20-employee startup** looking at n8n Business → check their Startup plan at -50% — often the best value on the market for growing technical teams.
