## The Real Cost of Delivery Platforms

Delivery apps have one thing going for them: they bring volume. But they charge a steep price for it — and that price goes beyond the commission.

| Criterion | Platform (Uber Eats / Deliveroo) | Direct website |
|---|---|---|
| Commission per order | 20 to 30% | ~1.4% + €0.25 (Stripe) |
| Customer data | Owned by the platform | **Yours** |
| Customer reviews | Hosted by the platform | **Yours** |
| Loyalty | Almost impossible | Built-in programme |
| Net margin | Cut on every order | Preserved |

On a €30 order, the platform takes roughly €9. The same payment on your own website costs €0.67. Multiply by your monthly volume and the maths is undeniable.

### The Numbers That Make the Decision

Take a restaurant doing **€5,000 of online sales per month**.

- Via a platform at 30%: ~€1,500 in monthly commission.
- Via a direct website with Stripe at 1.4%: ~€70 in fees.

The gap is around **€1,400 per month** — nearly **€17,000 per year**. At that rate, a transactional website pays for itself in weeks, not years.

## Why Your Website Has Become Non-Negotiable

Customer behaviour has shifted: **9 in 10 people check a restaurant on their phone before visiting**. Menu, opening hours, booking, reviews. If what they find is slow, outdated or non-existent, they move on.

A modern website is therefore not a luxury: it is your main shopfront, open 24/7, that turns a Google search into a reservation or an order.

## The Features That Actually Drive Revenue

### Click & Collect with Real-Time Tracking

The customer orders, pays online, chooses a collection slot and receives a **4-digit code**. In the kitchen, the order moves through three statuses visible to the customer: **Received → Being prepared → Ready**. No queue, no mistakes, no commission.

### Anti-Waste Promotions in One Click

End of service with unsold stock? Activate a **"last hour" or "today's leftover" promo (−20 to −50%)** in a single click. The price updates **live on the menu** your customers see. You clear the stock instead of throwing it away — and attract bargain-hunters who become regulars.

### Loyalty: The Most Underestimated Lever

Retaining a customer costs **around 5 times less** than acquiring a new one. A points programme (**€1 = 1 point**) with reward tiers — free coffee, dessert, dinner — gives regulars a concrete reason to come back.

The maths speaks for itself: if **300 regulars** return **one extra time per month** at **€25 average basket**, that's **+€7,500/month in recurring revenue** — with zero acquisition spend.

### Reviews and Social Proof

Online reviews and social proof can increase reservation rates by **30 to 50%**. On your own website, those reviews belong to you and work for you — not for a platform.
